Support

For help with My Wealth Square, email mywealthsquare22@gmail.com.

What to include

Please include your app version, Android version, phone model and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are useful when they do not contain sensitive financial information.

Google Play purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. If an eligible purchase does not appear after reopening the app, use the app's Restore Purchase function before contacting support.

Backups

Encrypted backup files are protected by the password you choose. The developer does not know or store that password and cannot recover an encrypted backup if the password is lost.

Privacy

Do not email account passwords, banking details, card information or complete personal financial records.
