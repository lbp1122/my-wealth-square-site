Support

Need help with My Wealth Square? Contact us for app installation, Free or Full Version access, Google Play purchase issues, backup and restore questions, reports, or general product feedback.

Email support

mywealthsquare22@gmail.com

For your privacy, do not email account passwords, banking details, card details or complete personal financial records.

Before contacting support

Please include the app version shown on your Android App info page, your phone model, Android version and a short description of what happened. Screenshots are useful when they do not contain sensitive financial information.

Google Play purchases

Full Version purchases are processed by Google Play. My Wealth Square does not receive your payment-card details. If an eligible purchase does not appear after reopening the app, use the app's restore-purchase function before contacting support.

Backups

Encrypted backup files are protected by the password you choose. The developer does not know or store that password and cannot recover an encrypted backup if the password is lost.

Educational use

My Wealth Square is an educational planning tool. Support can help explain how the app works, but does not provide personalised investment, tax, legal or financial-product advice.
