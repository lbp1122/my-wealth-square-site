Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 September 2026

My Wealth Square is an educational personal wealth planner and retirement-planning application. This policy explains how the Android app handles information.

Information you enter

No My Wealth Square account is required. Asset, liability, expense, retirement-planning, language and currency figures are stored locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, share or sell those financial figures.

Reports

Reports are generated on your device. A report leaves your device only when you choose to save or share it to another location or service.

Encrypted backups

You may create an encrypted backup containing My Wealth Square data. The backup is encrypted on your device using a password you choose before you select where to save it. My Wealth Square does not receive, store or know your backup password or backup file.

Google Play purchases

The app may offer a one-time Full Version purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes purchase and payment information under Google's terms and privacy practices. My Wealth Square does not receive or store your card or bank-account details.

Advertising, analytics and financial accounts

My Wealth Square does not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs and does not connect to your bank, brokerage or other financial accounts.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved app data remains on your device until you clear the app's data, reset the device, or uninstall the app. You control any PDF reports and encrypted backup files that you save. Deleting those files from their chosen storage location removes those copies.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email mywealthsquare22@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords, banking details, card details or complete personal financial records by email.
