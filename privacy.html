Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

My Wealth Square is an educational personal wealth blueprint and planning app intended for users aged 12 and above. Financial Life Planning calculations are intended for adults aged 18 and above. This policy explains how the website and Android and iOS/iPadOS apps handle information.

Your finances stay yours - private, local and available offline

No financial-data collection by the developer. No account required. No advertising or analytics SDK. Financial inputs and calculations remain on your device. These claims concern your financial figures, not technical information processed by third-party services you elect to use.

Information you enter

No account is required. Asset, liability, expense, retirement-planning, language and currency figures are saved locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, share or sell those figures.

Retirement simulation

The retirement simulator uses assumptions chosen by you to produce an educational projection. Its calculations run on your device and are not personalised financial advice, a guarantee, or an offer of a financial product.

PDF export and sharing

Reports are generated on your device. You can select password protection before generating a PDF report and choose a private password. The developer does not receive or know that password. Reports remain on your device unless you choose to save or share them elsewhere; once sent, the recipient and sharing platform may retain or forward a copy. Password protection reduces the risk of casual access but does not prevent an authorised recipient from copying, printing or screenshotting the content. Share the password separately from the PDF, preferably through another trusted channel.

Encrypted backups

You may choose to create an encrypted backup file containing your My Wealth Square data. The backup is encrypted on your device with a password you choose before you select a storage destination such as Google Drive, OneDrive or local storage. My Wealth Square does not receive, store or know your backup password or backup file. You must keep the password because the developer cannot recover an encrypted backup without it.

Android, iOS and website hosting

The Android and iOS/iPadOS apps package their calculator files on the device. Core wealth tracking, financial calculations, local reports and local saved-data review work without an internet connection. Some functions may need a connection, including Google Play or Apple App Store billing, purchases and entitlement refresh, software updates, accessing the public website and online exchange-rate information. The separate public website at https://lbp1122.github.io/my-wealth-square-site/ is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical request data to deliver and secure the website under its own privacy terms. Figures entered into My Wealth Square are not sent to GitHub by the calculator.

Google Play purchases and subscriptions

The Android app may offer Annual Full Access and a separate one-time Full Version purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment methods, charges, renewals, cancellations, refunds and applicable taxes under its own terms and privacy practices. The app receives only the purchase-entitlement information required to unlock or restore Full access; My Wealth Square does not receive or store your card or bank-account details.

Apple App Store purchases and subscriptions

The iOS/iPadOS app offers Annual Full Access and a separate one-time Full Version purchase through Apple's StoreKit and App Store. Apple handles payments, renewals, billing information, refunds and purchases under its own terms and privacy practices. The app receives the verified purchase entitlement and localized price information needed to offer, unlock and restore purchased access; the developer does not receive your credit-card or bank-account information. Subscription cancellation and management take place through Apple. Financial figures remain stored on your device and are not uploaded to the developer by purchase or restoration.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

My Wealth Square contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, financial-account connection or developer-operated user-tracking service. It does not offer loans, banking, trading, investment, insurance or payment services.

Fictional sample reports on the website

The demonstration reports are made entirely from invented names, accounts, references and amounts. They do not contain users' financial records. Public demonstration passwords are intended to explain how encrypted PDFs open, not to secure real private information.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved figures remain until you clear the app or browser data, reset your device, or uninstall the app. You control any PDF copies and encrypted backup files you save or share. An encrypted backup can be used to restore your data after reinstalling the app or moving to another device.

Age

My Wealth Square is intended for users aged 12 and above. Users aged 12–17 may use its educational and wealth-recording features with appropriate parent or guardian guidance. Financial Life Planning calculations, including retirement simulations and financial projections, are intended for adults aged 18 and above. These projections are educational estimates, not personalised financial advice. Purchases made by minors require applicable parent or guardian permission.

Contact

For privacy questions, email mywealthsquare22@gmail.com. Do not include account numbers, references or personal financial figures in an email.
