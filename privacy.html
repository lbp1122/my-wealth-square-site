Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

My Wealth Square is an educational personal wealth planner, net-worth calculator and retirement simulator developed by LBP. This policy explains how the public website and Android app handle information.

Information you enter

No account is required. Asset, liability, expense, retirement-planning, language and currency figures are saved locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, share or sell those figures.

Retirement simulation

The retirement simulator uses assumptions chosen by you to produce an educational projection. Its calculations are not personalised financial advice, a guarantee, or an offer of a financial product.

PDF export and sharing

Reports are generated on your device. A report leaves your device only when you choose a save or share destination. The privacy practices of that destination then apply.

Encrypted backups

You may choose to create an encrypted backup containing My Wealth Square data. The backup is encrypted on your device using a password you choose before you select a storage destination. My Wealth Square does not receive, store or know your backup password or backup file.

Android app and website hosting

The Android app packages the calculator files on the device and core calculations do not require internet access. This public concept website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical request data to deliver and secure the website under its own privacy terms. This website does not ask you to enter personal financial figures.

Google Play purchases

The Android app may offer a one-time Full Version upgrade through Google Play Billing. Google processes purchase and payment information under its own terms and privacy practices. My Wealth Square does not receive or store your card or bank-account details.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

My Wealth Square contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, financial-account connection or user-tracking service. It does not offer loans, banking, trading, investment, insurance or payment services.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved figures remain until you clear app data, reset your device, or uninstall the Android app. You control any PDF copies and encrypted backup files you save or share. An encrypted backup can be used to restore your data after reinstalling the app or moving to another device.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email mywealthsquare22@gmail.com. Please do not send personal financial figures, passwords, banking details or card details by email.
